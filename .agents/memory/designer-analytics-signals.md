---
name: Designer analytics signals
description: Rules for interpreting designer performance and client-experience reporting.
---

Designer analytics must show auditable workload and client-experience facts without a composite performance score, “best designer” label, or leaderboard.

**Why:** Rankings imply a quality judgment the underlying operational data cannot support. Filed complaints are allegations until confirmed, so only confirmed complaints represent a negative designer-quality signal.

**How to apply:** Keep orders, completion counts, cancellations, ratings, complaints, and suggestions as separate metrics. For period reporting, attribute completed work to its completion date rather than the order creation date.

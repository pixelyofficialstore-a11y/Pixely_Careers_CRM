---
name: Semantic status colors
description: The approved Pixely CRM convention for status and financial value colors.
---

Use semantic colors only for meaningful business states: green for completed, approved, paid, received, or resolved; red for canceled, refunded, rejected, outstanding, or payment due; amber for new, waiting, or confirmed; slate for neutral states. Keep normal IDs, names, dates, labels, descriptions, and totals calm and use Pixely cyan for interaction.

**Why:** The final stabilization direction preserves the dark navy/cyan theme and avoids turning every CRM value into a bright status signal.

**How to apply:** Reuse the shared status badge and accounting display rules in every table, drawer, dashboard, and report instead of defining page-specific color mappings.

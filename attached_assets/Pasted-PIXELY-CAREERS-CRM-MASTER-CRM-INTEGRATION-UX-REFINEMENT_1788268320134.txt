PIXELY CAREERS CRM
MASTER CRM INTEGRATION, UX REFINEMENT & CLIENT CASE REPORT TASK

==================================================
CORE OBJECTIVE
==================================================

The CRM now has working modules for:

- Orders
- Complaints
- Payments
- Client Feedback
  - Reviews
  - Suggestions
- Team
- Analytics
- Dashboard

The individual systems are beginning to work, but they still need to behave as ONE unified CRM.

This task is NOT primarily about adding random new modules.

This task is to:

1. Make every module interconnected.
2. Make Orders the central source of truth.
3. Make navigation between Orders, Complaints, Reviews, Suggestions and Payments seamless.
4. Make every status understandable.
5. Make every important action recorded.
6. Improve Order Activity into a complete audit/history timeline.
7. Allow Designers, Support and Admin to add Reviews and Suggestions.
8. Make role permissions consistent.
9. Create a complete exportable "Client Case Report".
10. Improve UI consistency and remove unnecessary or confusing elements.
11. Preserve all existing functionality.
12. Make the CRM feel like one professionally designed system.

IMPORTANT:

DO NOT rebuild the CRM.
DO NOT replace working modules.
DO NOT reset Supabase.
DO NOT rewrite authentication.
DO NOT change Cloudinary configuration.
DO NOT delete working Complaints, Reviews, Suggestions, Payments or Orders.
DO NOT perform unrelated refactoring.

FIRST inspect all existing code and functionality.

SECTION 9 — REVIEW SHOULD HAVE A CLEAR LIFECYCLE
==================================================

Do NOT create confusing meaningless statuses.

Use:

Review Progress

Possible values:

Requested
Received
Public Review Received
Closed

MEANINGS:

REQUESTED

The team has requested client feedback but the client has not yet provided actual feedback.

RECEIVED

Client feedback/rating has been received and recorded.

PUBLIC REVIEW RECEIVED

At least one public/marketing review has been received, such as:

Facebook Review
or
Video Testimonial where treated as public/marketing proof.

CLOSED

Review collection process has been completed and no more review follow-up is required.

==================================================
REVIEW PROGRESS AUTOMATION
==================================================

Do not require unnecessary manual updates.

Automatically move Requested → Received.

Allow/automatically move to Public Review Received.

Closed:
May be manually selected when the review collection process is finished.

Do not automatically move backward.

Admin may correct mistakes if necessary.

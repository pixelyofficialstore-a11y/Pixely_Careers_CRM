Measure each designer’s daily, monthly, and overall productivity based on completed work.

  Working → Ready
- The date of this status change is stored as the **Completion Date**
- ALL designer performance calculations must be based on Completion Date

METRICS PER DESIGNER:
- Orders Completed Today
- Orders Completed This Month
- Total Orders Completed
- Pending Orders
- Canceled Orders (assigned to that designer)

REPORT VIEWS:
- Daily Performance Report (designer-wise)
- Monthly Performance Report (designer-wise)
- Overall Performance Summary

TABLE COLUMNS (Designer Analytics):
- Designer Name
- Orders Completed Today
- Orders Completed This Month
- Pending Orders
- Canceled Orders
- Average Daily Completion Rate

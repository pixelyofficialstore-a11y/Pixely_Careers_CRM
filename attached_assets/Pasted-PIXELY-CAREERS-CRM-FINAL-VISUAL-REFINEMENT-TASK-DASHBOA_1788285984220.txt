==================================================
VERY IMPORTANT FINANCE LOGIC
==================================================

Do NOT blindly calculate:

Collected - Refunds

if the current "Collected" metric already excludes refunded payments.

Inspect the current finance architecture first.

Create a correct single-source cash-flow calculation.

Conceptually:

Gross Cash Inflow
=
actual approved payments received during the period

Cash Outflow
=
actual refunds recorded during the period

Net Cash Flow
=
Gross Cash Inflow - Refunds

This must NOT double-deduct refunds.

==================================================
CANCELED ORDER RULE
==================================================

Previously approved rules remain:

Canceled + advance refunded:

Net retained cash from that advance = 0

Canceled + advance NOT refunded:

Advance remains part of retained cash.

Remaining canceled balance never counts as cash inflow.
